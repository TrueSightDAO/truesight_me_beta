The Perch

A Steward's View of the Markets

The Perch is a market steward dashboard — a place to observe, interpret, and understand the economic context around the DAO's mission. It tracks sentiment signals, backtests strategies, and watches market conditions that affect the rainforest, the farmers, and the community.

The challenge

Markets Move. The Mission Stays.

Protecting 10,000 hectares of Amazon rainforest requires more than good intentions — it requires understanding the economic forces that shape the land. Cacao prices, currency fluctuations, supply chain costs, and market sentiment all affect farmers, buyers, and the ecosystem. Without a clear view of these forces, decisions are guesses.

The solution

See the Patterns. Understand the Context.

The Perch brings together market data, sentiment analysis, and historical backtesting into a single vantage point. It doesn't trade. It watches. From here, patterns emerge that inform better decisions for the rainforest, the farmers, and the community.

Sentiment Dashboard

Track market sentiment signals across tickers and sectors. See how the market feels about the industries that matter to the mission.

Backtester

Test trading strategies against historical data. Compare buy-and-hold vs rebalance bands. Understand what works before committing capital.

Market Compare

Overlay sentiment and price data across tickers. See correlations at a glance. Spot divergences before they become trends.

How it works

Data In, Insight Out

01

Collect

The Krake engine gathers news headlines, price data, and sentiment signals from global markets.

02

Analyze

Sentiment analysis extracts noun phrases and correlates them with price movements over time.

03

Present

The Perch dashboard visualizes the data so you can see patterns, test strategies, and make informed decisions.

Ecosystem

Part of Something Bigger

The Perch is one piece of the TrueSight DAO ecosystem. It works alongside Edgar (the DAO Protocol) to connect market intelligence with on-the-ground action.
